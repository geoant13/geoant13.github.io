I’m a PhD student in natural language processing at the AILS Lab of the National Technical University of Athens. I study how large language models represent, store and edit what they know, and I build open resources for Greek public-sector text.

Research

Knowledge inside language models

My MSc thesis, Priors, Scaling, and Redefinition of Constants in LLMs, supervised by Assoc. Prof. Athanasios Voulodimos, built a benchmark of 43 constants and used it to study 14 open-source models: how firmly they hold on to values they learned in pre-training, how that changes with scale, and how well they reason once a constant is redefined.

In my PhD I’m following the same question further into the model, starting with model-editing experiments using ROME on Qwen3 models.

Greek public-sector documents

Διαύγεια (Diavgeia) is the Greek government’s transparency platform, where every public body publishes its decisions, mostly as PDFs. Building on my undergraduate work at the University of Thessaly, I turned about one million of these decisions into an open, machine-readable dataset, with a reproducible extraction pipeline and a retrieval-augmented question-answering task for evaluating systems on it.

At the Open Technologies Alliance (EELLAK) I built on this with Diavgeia Assistant, an open-source assistant that answers questions about public decisions in Greek and links each answer to the decisions it draws on.
